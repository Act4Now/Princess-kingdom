WHAT CHANGED
1. The active-player indicator moved into the scoreboard panel position. If
   multiple profiles exist, the active profile is displayed there. In Team Mode,
   it appears above the team scores.
2. In Team Mode, “Altă provocare” changes to “Nu am reușit”. Pressing it moves
   to the next team and next challenge without awarding a point to the team that
   did not complete the challenge. The team score remains unchanged.
